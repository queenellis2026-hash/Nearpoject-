Paid in Full

A contractor invoice-to-payment desk on NEAR (testnet design, local escrow simulation). Purple = the agent clerk acts. Blue = a human must act.

1. The job & the approved contract sum

The homeowner and the crew agreed a contract sum up front. The clerk may never pay past it.

2. Agent clerk prepares the invoice

3. Resolve & human approval

If the clerk flags an overage, a human either corrects the invoice or approves a change order that raises the approved sum. The clerk then re-checks. Nothing is payable before a human approves.

4. Payment — escrow on NEAR (simulated locally)

Two steps, as on-chain: first the approved invoice is funded into escrow, then the funding client releases it to the crew. The escrow contract enforces the same order on NEAR testnet — funding requires the client's approval first.
